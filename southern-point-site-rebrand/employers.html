STAFFING SOLUTIONS

Different needs call for different engagements.

Southern Point structures every engagement around the role and timeline, not a single fixed service.

01

Temporary Staffing

Short-term coverage for leave, seasonal volume, or unexpected gaps — without a permanent commitment.

02

Temp-to-Hire

Bring a candidate on temporarily and convert to a permanent hire once you've confirmed the fit.

03

Direct Hire

Southern Point sources and screens candidates for permanent roles you hire directly.

04

Contract Staffing

Project-based and fixed-term engagements for defined scopes of work.

05

High-Volume Staffing

Coordinated sourcing and screening when you need to fill multiple similar roles at once.

06

Workforce Solutions

Ongoing, flexible workforce support structured around how your organization actually operates.
