# Southern Point — Photography Asset Manifest

This site's visual design is built photography-first: every slot below is a real
`<img>` element in the HTML, sized and positioned exactly as the final photo will
sit. Until licensed photography is supplied, each slot renders a plain neutral
placeholder (flat stone-gray field, no pattern, no gradient, no decoration) purely
as a development stand-in — it is intentionally *not* part of the design language.

**To replace an image:** drop the finished file into `assets/images/` under the
exact filename listed below, matching (or exceeding) the recommended dimensions.
No code changes are required — every slot already points at its final filename.

General direction for every image on this list unless a slot says otherwise:

- Style: contemporary, editorial/documentary rather than staged. Natural light
  where plausible. Real working moments, not posed "look at camera" shots.
- Avoid everywhere: handshake photos, people pointing at laptops, exaggerated
  smiling-at-camera stock poses, obvious AI-generated people, watermarks,
  low-resolution or upscaled images, generic "success" imagery, cheap/flat
  corporate stock photography.
- Color: photography should sit comfortably against Southern Point's palette
  (Midnight #15171E, Near-black #0F1015, Burgundy #6E2436, Bronze #B8863E /
  #D8B67A, Cream #F3EEE3) — warm, natural tones read best; avoid heavy blue or
  teal color grading that will clash.
- Do not depict Southern Point's actual staff, clients, or offices unless the
  photo genuinely is that — never caption stock photography as if it shows real
  employees or clients.

## Homepage (`/`)

### 1. `hero-workforce.jpg`
- **Section:** Hero, right half of a two-column split (text left, image right)
- **Dimensions:** 1600×2000 minimum (portrait/tall crop), source at 2400px+ on the long edge
- **Orientation:** Portrait or a tall landscape that can be cropped to portrait
- **Subject:** A small, real-feeling group (2–4 people) mid-conversation or
  mid-task in a modern professional workplace — could read as technology,
  healthcare admin, operations, or office/administrative work (Southern Point's
  five focus industries), not any single industry in particular
- **Composition:** Natural depth, some negative space in the upper or lower
  third for the section to breathe against the dark hero background; subject
  should not be dead-center/symmetrical — asymmetric, editorial framing
- **Negative space:** None required on the image itself (headline sits in the
  left column, not overlaid on this photo)
- **Avoid:** call-center imagery, a single person staring at the camera,
  visible laptop screens/UI (looks dated fast), stock-photo lighting

### 2. `homepage-brand.jpg`
- **Section:** Large full-bleed photographic section, lower homepage, paired
  with the "trajectory" brand statement
- **Dimensions:** 2800×1400 minimum (wide landscape, full-bleed)
- **Orientation:** Landscape, wide
- **Subject:** A wider environmental shot — a modern workplace, a team in
  motion, a hallway/office in use — something that reads as "organizations at
  work," not a specific department
- **Composition:** Full-bleed edge to edge; should hold up cropped very short
  (the section is more letterbox than square) — keep the subject roughly
  centered vertically with room to crop top/bottom
- **Negative space:** Keep the horizontal center third relatively uncluttered
  in case a short line of type is later overlaid
- **Avoid:** empty/sterile stock-office shots with no people in them

### 3. `homepage-employers.jpg`
- **Section:** "For Organizations" split section (paired with the Find Talent CTA)
- **Dimensions:** 1600×1200 minimum
- **Orientation:** Landscape or square-ish (will sit in one half of a 2-column split)
- **Subject:** A hiring-manager/decision-maker moment — reviewing something
  together, a working meeting — signals the employer side of the business
- **Avoid:** interview-across-a-desk clichés, exaggerated handshakes

### 4. `homepage-candidates.jpg`
- **Section:** "For Job Seekers" split section (paired with the Find Jobs CTA)
- **Dimensions:** 1600×1200 minimum
- **Orientation:** Landscape or square-ish
- **Subject:** Should read candidate-side — someone starting a new role,
  onboarding, a genuine moment of someone at work — optimistic but not staged
- **Avoid:** graduation-cap/resume-on-a-clipboard clichés

### 16. `about-story.jpg`

- **Dimensions:** 1800×2200 minimum (portrait) or 2400×1400 (landscape) —

## Reuse notes

Southern Point's own direction is "fewer images, but make every image count" —
if sourcing all 17 unique photos isn't practical right away, the priority order
is: `hero-workforce.jpg` → `homepage-brand.jpg` → `about-story.jpg` →
`services-staffing.jpg` → `industries-hub.jpg`. Every other slot can
temporarily reuse one of those five (matching mood/orientation) and be
upgraded to a unique photo later — each slot is an independent file, so
upgrading one doesn't touch any code.
