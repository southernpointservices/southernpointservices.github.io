HOW WE WORK

Engagement models built around you.

01

Temporary

Short-term coverage for leave, seasonal volume, or unexpected gaps — without a permanent commitment.

02

Temp-to-Hire

Bring a candidate on temporarily and convert to a permanent hire once you've confirmed the fit.

03

Direct Hire

Southern Point sources and screens candidates for permanent roles you hire directly.

04

Contract

Project-based and fixed-term engagements for defined scopes of work.

WHY SOUTHERN POINT

Not a high-volume staffing mill.

Specific.

Candidates sourced against the actual role, not a generic applicant pool run through a keyword filter.

Screened.

Every candidate is screened for relevant experience and fit before you ever see a resume.

Responsive.

A streamlined process means less time between identifying a need and having qualified people to consider.

Engaged.

Southern Point stays engaged after placement rather than disappearing once someone starts.
